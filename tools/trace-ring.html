Trace a ring road

This asks OpenStreetMap for the motorway's own centreline — the ways it is built from — and stitches them into the loop the road makes. That is the road itself, with no routing involved, so there are no slip roads or turnarounds in the answer.

It runs in your browser, on your network.

A road number is not unique to one country — there is an M25 in Cape Town as well as round London — so the search is confined to 150 km of the point given. Latitude, then longitude.
