Review the borders

Three cities are drawn by hand and checked by tests. Every other border is whatever OpenStreetMap publishes under the name, and no automatic check can tell whether it is where a resident would say the town ends — only a resident can. This shows them one at a time and writes down what you say.

Put a name in before answering — a verdict nobody can be asked about afterwards is not much use, and it keeps two people on one machine apart.

Answers stay in this browser until you send them. They are not saved anywhere else: a different browser, a different machine, a private window or a cleared cache all start empty. Press Send my answers when you are done, or partway through — it downloads a small file to pass on.
