Trace an area

Some boundaries are not roads. The Greater Toronto Area is a set of municipalities, and OpenStreetMap publishes each one, so this asks for them by name and puts them together. Where two of them meet, the line between them is dropped, leaving the outline round the whole. Nothing is drawn by hand and nothing is traced from a picture.

One area per line, named as the map names it.

York and Durham are named town by town rather than whole: the regions run north to Lake Simcoe and east past Bowmanville, and the boundary wanted here does not.
